Barabar Privacy Policy

Smart Tips. Simple Splits.

Effective date: November 6, 2026  |  Last updated: November 5, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Barabar app (package name com.scentavenuellc.barabar), published by Scent Avenue LLC ("we", "us", "our"), handles information. Barabar is a tip calculator and bill splitter for Android.

In short: Barabar does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

2. Information we do not collect

Barabar does not require an account or login, and we do not collect any of the following:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers or other contact details
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data
  • Device identifiers or advertising identifiers
  • Usage analytics, crash reports or diagnostics
  • Payment or financial account information

3. Data stored on your device

To work properly, the app stores the following information locally on your device only, using Android's private app storage (SharedPreferences):

  • Your settings: theme (dark or light), currency symbol, default tip percentage and the round-up option
  • Your saved calculations (bill amount, tip, total, number of people and amount per person), up to 50 entries

This data never leaves your device. You can delete individual entries or clear the whole history at any time inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes all of this data.

4. Internet access and permissions

Barabar works completely offline. The app does not declare the internet permission and does not request any other Android permissions.

5. Clipboard

When you tap the Copy button, the app places the text of your calculation result on your device clipboard. The app does not read your clipboard and does not send it anywhere.

6. Third parties, ads and tracking

Barabar contains no advertising, no analytics tools, no third-party SDKs that collect data, and no tracking of any kind. We do not sell, rent, trade or share any information with third parties.

7. Children's privacy

Barabar does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because no data is collected, the app is suitable for general audiences.

8. Data security

Your data is kept in the private storage area of the app on your device, which is protected by the Android operating system. Because we do not operate any servers for this app, we do not hold your data.

9. Your rights and choices

Since we do not collect or store your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You are in full control of the locally stored data and can remove it at any time from within the app or by clearing the app storage or uninstalling the app.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the app gains new features. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
